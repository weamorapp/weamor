We Amor – Child Safety & Anti-Exploitation Policy

At We Amor, we are committed to maintaining a safe and respectful environment for all users. We enforce a zero-tolerance policy toward any form of Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE), including the distribution, promotion, or possession of Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM).

Our Commitments

  • Zero Tolerance: Any CSAE-related activity will result in immediate ban and reporting to authorities.
  • Proactive Detection: We use automated tools and human moderation to detect and block harmful content.
  • User Reporting: In-app features allow users to report suspicious activity, prioritized within 24 hours.
  • Law Enforcement: We cooperate with global and local agencies on CSAE-related incidents.
  • Safety by Design: We Amor is strictly for users aged 18+. Age checks and moderation are enforced.
